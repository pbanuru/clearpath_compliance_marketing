# Project brief and context

## Assignment supplied by the company

The six bullets under **Take Home Project - 24h** in [README.md](../README.md)
are the original assignment. They are preserved verbatim there. The material
below those bullets in Pranav's pasted notes is his own working context, not
additional company requirements.

The assignment asks for a product that increases throughput of ClearPath
Financial's compliance marketing team, whose current process uses Excel and
email. ClearPath is fictional; reasonable assumptions are allowed.

## Pranav's working notes

These are interpretations and questions to develop, rather than validated
customer research or requirements added to the assignment:

- National means operating across a country. Consumer means serving individuals.
- The listed products are personal loans, credit cards, and mortgage
  prequalification.
- Someone might choose ClearPath over a bank for accessibility, a better deal,
  speed, or a company that seems to understand them. These are possible reasons,
  not established facts about the fictional business.
- The primary users work in an internal compliance marketing department.
- Marketing compliance covers applicable external rules and internal policies;
  requirements can differ by product, channel, and jurisdiction.
- Affiliate partners matter because the review problem includes materials
  marketed by partners as well as materials created internally.
- Marketing channels and the actual review workflow need research.

## Unresolved product questions

- Which channels and material formats best demonstrate the review problem?
- What context does a reviewer need before making a decision?
- Which friction should the product remove: incomplete submissions, finding
  issues, communicating changes, comparing versions, or tracking decisions?
- How should affiliate-created material enter the same review process?

The prompt establishes a throughput bottleneck, but does not establish its
cause. Any chosen cause and workflow remain explicit assumptions for this
fictional-company exercise.
