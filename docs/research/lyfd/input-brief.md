# ClearPath Marketing Compliance: product opportunity

Build a product that increases throughput for ClearPath Financial's internal
compliance marketing team. Research should establish how comparable teams do
this work, what slows them down, and what they need from a product. The intended
user is the compliance marketing team, rather than consumers shopping for
loans or credit cards. This is a product opportunity brief.

## Original assignment

**Take Home Project - 24h**

- ClearPath Financial (fictional company) is a national consumer finance company that offers personal loans, credit cards, and mortgage prequalification products to consumers online.
- The company markets these products through typical marketing channels, including affiliate partners.
- The compliance marketing team’s review process which is currently done via Excel and email at the company is currently bottlenecking growth
- You are tasked with building a product that increases throughput of the compliance marketing team.
- Feel free to make reasonable assumptions
- Final Deliverable: URL to deployed project and Github Repo

## Explicit working assumption

ClearPath operates nationally in the United States. The assignment does not
specify a country; this is a working assumption chosen for this exercise.

## Evidence boundary

ClearPath is fictional. Its actual team size, review volume, marketing channels,
formats, policies, workflow, and cause of the bottleneck are unknown. There
are no ClearPath customer interviews or usage measurements. External evidence
can illuminate comparable teams and practices; it cannot establish facts
about this fictional company's operations.
