# Initial marketing review research

## Scope and provenance

This is external research, separate from the assignment and Pranav's notes in
[project-brief.md](project-brief.md). It does not establish how ClearPath's
fictional team actually works. US jurisdiction is a working assumption; the
assignment says national without specifying a country.

## What the sources establish

- Credit advertising rules differ between open-end products, such as credit
  cards, and closed-end products. Advertised terms must actually be available.
  Some advertised terms trigger additional disclosures. Review context therefore
  needs the product and the terms being advertised.
  Sources: [CFPB Regulation Z §1026.16](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/16/)
  and [§1026.24](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/24/).
- Placement and prominence can matter as well as wording. A text-only review
  can miss how a disclosure appears beside the claim in the finished material.
  Source: [CFPB §1026.24(b)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/24/).
- Paid endorsements and affiliate recommendations can require clear disclosure
  of the relationship. Whether a disclosure is necessary depends on the actual
  presentation; an obvious paid ad is different from an apparently independent
  recommendation. Source: [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).

## Implications to explore

These are product hypotheses, not findings from interviews:

- A reviewer should see the material's presentation alongside its product,
  channel, author or affiliate, and advertised offer.
- Reviewer feedback should identify the actual claim or disclosure at issue.
- Revised submissions could benefit from showing what changed and whether
  earlier feedback was addressed, rather than requiring another full review.

Candidate formats to investigate include ads, landing pages, marketing emails,
and affiliate comparison or recommendation pages. Supporting every format,
monitoring every partner website, and automating a complete legal determination
are not requirements in the assignment.
